1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies like local storage and session storage to keep you logged in and make Misfitz Games work properly.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in and maintain your session
  • Remember your preferences (e.g., display name, room code, UI settings)
  • Improve performance and reliability (e.g., error logging, basic analytics if enabled)
  • Protect the service (fraud prevention, abuse detection, rate limiting)
3. Types of cookies we may use
  • Strictly Necessary — required for login, security, and core features (cannot be switched off)
  • Functional — remember preferences (e.g., last room, UI options)
  • Analytics (optional) — helps us understand usage and improve the service
4. Example cookies / storage used

The exact names may change as we update the platform, but typically include:

  • Auth/session cookie — keeps you logged in
  • CSRF / security token cookie — protects form submissions
  • Local storage keys — e.g., last room code, UI preferences
5. Managing cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings and delete existing cookies. If you disable strictly necessary cookies, parts of Misfitz Games may not work (including login and multiplayer rooms).

6. Third-party services

If you connect third-party services (e.g., Twitch/TikTok/Discord or device providers like Tuya), those providers may set their own cookies or process data under their policies.
